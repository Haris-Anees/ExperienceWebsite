Introduction/Abstract

During my Summer 2025 co-op term, I worked as a Database Developer at the Arrell Food Institute in Guelph, Ontario.

My main focus was on building and managing a Azure SQL database containing over 900 learning assets related to food systems and sustainability.

In order to get these courses, I relied on various python libraries such as BeautifulSoup and Selenium in order to efficiently scrape courses.

Information about the Employer

During my co-op at the Arrell Food Institute, I worked with Jeanna Rex, Education Lead, and Dr. Amy Proulx, a Faculty Research Lead, Culinary Innovation and Food Technology at Niagara College . They have deep experience in agriculture, and our daily stand-up meetings helped me understand project goals, clarify tasks, and stay on track. Working with them gave me a better sense of how agriculture and sustainability projects are run in practice.

Job description

As a Database Developer, my responsibilities included designing a Azure SQL database to store and organize 900+ courses and learning assets. These learning assests included a micro-credentials, certificates, and Online Educational Resources (OERs). I developed Python scripts using BeautifulSoup and Selenium to automatically scrape course data from institutional websites. I also worked with my supervisors in daily Scrum meetings to track progress and work out deliverables. The job required skills in Python, SQL problem-solving, and communication skills.
